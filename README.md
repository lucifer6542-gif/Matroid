## A Square Basis Theorem for Matroids

[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.5281%2Fzenodo.24135970-blue)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24135970)

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.24135970](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24135970) · 11 November 2026

For every matroid M of rank at least one, the square relations, that is, the exponent vectors of the symmetric exchange binomials, contain a ℤ-basis of the integer kernel ker<sub>ℤ</sub> A<sub>M</sub> of the basis incidence matrix. Equivalently, over any field the toric ideal I<sub>M</sub> becomes, after inverting the variables, a complete intersection of codim I<sub>M</sub> symmetric exchange binomials. The proof is constructive and combinatorial, and it uses no computer calculation. Uniform matroids admit an explicit unitriangular square basis, and the theorem yields a Plücker-ratio criterion for when two real matrices differ by an invertible row transformation and an invertible column scaling.
